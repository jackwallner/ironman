IM Iron Splits

Type your name once. Every race you have finished loads with full splits, bib numbers and division places — then gets ranked the way you actually think about it.

What it does

Your results are already published. They are just scattered across a different page for every race, and none of them can tell you which of your fourteen halves held your fastest bike.

Your splits, ranked

Every race ranked by swim, bike, run, or time lost in transition. Personal bests marked on each leg, with the gap from every other race.

Where you landed

Each split against the field that actually raced it. A rank means nothing without the size of the field, so you get both.

Race resume

Date, distance, bib, official time and division place for every start. Share it as text or a PDF in one tap.

Race notes

The conditions, nutrition and gear that produced each result, kept with the result.

Free and Pro

Searching, claiming your results, and your three most recent races with full splits are free. Iron Splits+ unlocks your complete history, the split leaderboards, field percentiles, race notes, the resume export and the full Pointers library.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Privacy

No account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK. Your race notes never leave your phone. The app reads the same public results pages anyone can open in a browser.

Privacy Terms Support

IM Iron Splits is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any race organiser or timing company. Results are shown as published by each event's timer.
