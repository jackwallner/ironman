Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

The short version

IM Iron Splits has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to us, because there is no server of ours for it to go to.

What stays on your device

  • The athlete you claimed, and a cached copy of your published race results.
  • Your race notes — conditions, nutrition, gear, and anything else you type. These are never uploaded.
  • Your unit preference and other settings.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. "Remove my athlete" in Settings clears the cached results immediately.

What leaves your device

Race results

When you search for your name or open a race, the app requests published results from the event timing service's public results endpoint. That request contains the search term or the identifier of the athlete or event being looked up. It does not contain your name unless your name is what you searched for, and it carries no device identifier, no advertising identifier, and no account.

Configuration

The app fetches two small JSON files from this site — one describing where the results service lives, one listing the coaching clips. These are ordinary web requests. This site is served by GitHub Pages, whose logs we do not have access to.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which records an anonymous identifier and your subscription status so your purchase can be restored on your other devices. It does not receive your name, your email, or your race results. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

IM Iron Splits is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
