IM Iron Splits Support

Email jackwallner+tri@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app.

The app cannot find me

Results are published under the name you registered with, which is often a full legal first name rather than the one people call you. Try that first. If many people share the name, add a city or state after a comma, such as “Jordan Smith, Madison.” A broader name search can take around 30 seconds and can be stopped at any time.

If a race is under another registration name, open Settings, Athlete, Find another registered name in the app. Search that name and select the matching athlete to add those published races to your existing Locker.

A race is missing

IM Iron Splits shows exactly what the timing service publishes. If a result is missing or wrong there, it will be missing or wrong here, and the fix has to come from the event. Very recent races can take a few days to appear.

Which races are covered

Published full-distance and half-distance triathlon results available from the supported timing service. Other race types in that feed, and races timed by other services, are not shown.

My splits look wrong

Compare against the official results page for that race. If they match, the app is reporting what it was given. If they do not, email the race, the year, and your bib number so the issue can be investigated.

Race Book and restores

Search, your full history, every split, personal bests, progression, rankings, field context, race notes, race details and the whole Pointers library are free. Race Book is a one-time lifetime purchase for like-for-like comparison between two races and unlimited PDF or image exports. There is no subscription and no per-export charge.

On a new device, or after reinstalling, tap Settings, Restore Purchases inside the app while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When you contact us

Include your device model, iOS version, the race and year, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots help. Do not send payment details or your Apple Account password.
