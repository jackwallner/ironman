## What does IM Tri Tracker do?

Find the split, understand the day it came from, and keep the record
somewhere you can actually use it.

### Find your races by name

Type the name you registered with and pick yourself out of the
official results index. If one race went in under a maiden name or a
misspelling, add that registration too and it joins the same locker.

### Every start in one place

Browse your published full and half-distance triathlon history with
dates, bibs, official times and division places. Switch between
races and split rankings, and spot personal bests without opening
every result.

### Swim, T1, bike, T2, run

Open a race and read the whole day leg by leg, with the overall and
division rank each split earned. Zeroed legs from a DNF stay empty
instead of pretending to be a fast one.

### Know where you landed

Each leg shows its place out of everyone who raced it, and the
percentile that follows.

### Rankings and progression, free

Rank finishes, swims, bikes, runs and transitions within one
distance at a time. Full and half-distance results never compete on
the same list. Review personal bests and see results from the same
event across years when you have them.

### Look up anyone else

Search another athlete and read their full and half distance history
the same way, for a training partner, a rival, or the person who
just beat you. Your own locker is untouched.

### Remember the day

Conditions, nutrition, gear and what you would do differently, kept
on the result that produced them. Notes stay on the phone and only
leave it if you put them in an export.

### Pattie in your pocket

Pattie's filmed pointers and a guided path to the right one, free
with the app. Turn on Pattie Mode and she turns up in the corner
with a relevant tip while you browse, in her own recorded voice.

### Compare and export

One lifetime purchase adds like-for-like comparison between any two
races at the same distance, showing the time gained or lost on every
leg, plus unlimited polished PDF and image exports built on the
phone. No subscription, no per-export charge.

### Set up the way you like

Metric or imperial, light or dark or system, and haptics you can
adjust. Watched episodes are cached so they replay offline, and you
can clear them whenever you want the space.

### Clear about the source

Results are shown exactly as each event's timing provider published
them. The app sorts and compares that record, it never verifies or
rewrites it.

### Works after the signal drops

Your locker, your notes and the tips you have already watched are
all on the phone. Transition areas are famous for having no bars.

## Does IM Tri Tracker need an account?

No account, sign-in or advertising SDK. Name searches and public
result identifiers go to the event timing service to retrieve results.
Your cached races, notes and explored profiles stay on your iPhone.
When a Race Book purchase screen appears, anonymous purchase-flow
counts are sent to RevenueCat with purchase and restore information.
No race notes or results are sent to RevenueCat.

## More info

A simpler way to keep a long race history useful.

### The Pitch

An official results page tells you what happened in one race. It
does not tell you which of your starts held your fastest bike, how
much time transitions have quietly cost you over a decade, or
where a split landed in the field that actually raced it. Finding
any of that means opening a dozen tabs and doing arithmetic.

With IM Tri Tracker you type your name once, and a
career of published results becomes a locker you can search, rank,
annotate and share. Start with the name, then let the app do the
sorting.

### Race Book

Almost everything is free. Search, your
complete history, every split, personal bests, progression,
leaderboards, field percentiles, race details, notes and the whole
Pointers library cost nothing and are never hidden behind a
purchase.

The one paid boundary is Race Book: like-for-like comparison
between two races at the same distance, and unlimited PDF or image
exports of the career record. It is a single lifetime purchase
with no subscription and no per-export charge, and Restore
Purchases is there for a new phone or a reinstall.

### What Mattered

**Splits first.** A best finish is useful.
Knowing which race held your best bike, and how far off it you are
now, is the reason to come back.

**Full and half kept apart.** Full and half distance are
ranked in their own groups rather than flattened into one
flattering list, and a result whose claimed distance disagrees
with its own splits gets corrected.

**Your race history stays local.** There is no
account or developer-owned history server. Search terms go to the
timing service. RevenueCat receives purchase and restore details
plus anonymous purchase-screen counts, never your race results or
notes.

**Advice from a person.** The tips are filmed clips
from one athlete's own race days, not generated text. She can only
tell you something she actually said.

**The official record stays official.** The app makes
published results easier to use. The event's timing provider
remains the source of truth.

## Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the app.

## Is there an app to look up triathlon race results by name?

Yes. IM Tri Tracker finds published full and half-distance triathlon results from the name you registered with, and shows swim, T1, bike, T2 and run splits, bib numbers and division places.

## How can I compare my triathlon splits?

Open a race to see the overall and division rank of each split, with its place and percentile among everyone who raced it. Full and half-distance results are ranked in separate lists, so a half is never compared with a full.

## Is IM Tri Tracker free?

Yes. Search, your full history, splits, rankings, personal bests, progression, race notes and the Pattie tips library are free. An optional one-time Race Book purchase adds comparison between two races and unlimited PDF or image exports, with no subscription, and the price is shown in the app before you buy and varies by region.

## Can I look up another athlete's triathlon results?

Yes. Explore lets you search another athlete and read their full and half-distance history the same way, without changing your own locker.

## Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, sign-in or advertising SDK. Name searches go to the event timing service to retrieve results, and your cached races and notes stay on your phone.

## Are the results official?

The app shows results exactly as each event's timing provider published them, and it never rewrites them. It is an independent app, not affiliated with any race organizer or timing company, so check the official result when accuracy matters.

## Is there an Android version?

Yes. IM Tri Tracker is available for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android (8 or later).
