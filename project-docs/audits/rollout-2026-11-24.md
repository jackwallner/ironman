## Star prompt: the moment and why

Before: one trigger, opening a race detail that was a personal best on any leg including transitions. It only posted a notification; `RootTabView` called `requestReview()` instantly (no delay, no check for a sheet) once five launches, seven days, three such opens and three separate days of use had passed, with a 120-day cooldown. Classified: a real good result, but asked instantly and behind gates far stricter than the fleet's evidence supports. Sending feedback ended the asks for good, and Settings' write-review row never ended them.

The moment now: **the athlete opens one of their own finishes that is a personal best on the finish, swim, bike or run for its distance** (at least two races of that distance, so an only race is nobody's best). It is the app's core action ending in its best outcome: they are looking at their fastest race. Transition-only bests, DNFs, another racer's career in Explore, and the race the unfolded Duo pane shows without being picked never ask. It is the only automatic trigger.

How it asks (`IronSplits/Views/ReviewRequest.swift`, Mahj's `requestsReview(after:isClear:)` pattern): 3 seconds after the race opens, re-checks that the race is still on screen, the note editor is not open, and no window has a sheet, alert, cover or the paywall presented; otherwise that moment is skipped and the next one asks. Gates (`ReviewPromptTracker`): never during onboarding, not before the 2nd cold launch, 30 days between calls stamped when the call is made, opening Settings' "Write a review on the App Store" ends the asks, sending feedback never does (and never overwrites a write-review). No enjoyment question, no pre-prompt, no button. Settings keeps its write-review row, which deep-links to `?action=write-review`. The app has no analytics capture call, so there is no `rating_asked` event. AGENTS.md's review line names the new moment.

Upgrade: the tracker keeps its UserDefaults keys, so an existing install keeps its launch count, last ask date and a write-review that already ended the asks. Old `submittedFeedback` and `feedbackDraftOpened` outcomes no longer block the prompt (the rollout's rule: feedback never ends the asks).

## Frequency follow-up (Rollout R)

The goal is the number of ratings. The only moment was a personal best, which most athletes never reach in their first weeks (it needs a second race of the same distance that beats the first), so the prompt rarely fired. Commit: `feat: also ask for a rating when the athlete opens any of their own finishes`.

| Moment | Predicate | How often a typical user hits it |
|---|---|---|
| Opens one of their own completed races (new, the common moment) | `ReviewPromptTracker.isOwnFinishOpened`: own career (not another racer's, not the Duo's quiet default pane), `result.isComplete` | Nearly everyone who claims a profile opens a race in their first sessions, so by the 2nd or 3rd session (the 2nd cold launch gate) this is reached by most users. This replaces the old requirement of a personal best. |
| Opens a personal best on finish, swim, bike or run (kept as a rarer extra) | `ReviewPromptTracker.isPersonalBestOpened`: the above plus a best for its distance, at least two races of that distance | Only athletes with two or more races of one distance; a minority in the first week. It is now a subset of the common moment, so it adds no extra asks, and is kept as its own predicate in case the common moment is ever tightened. |

DNFs, DNS, DQs, another racer's career in Explore and the race the unfolded Duo pane shows unpicked never ask. All gates and the never-over-a-sheet check are unchanged: no ask before the 2nd cold launch, 30 days between calls stamped when the call is made, 3 second delay then a re-check that the race is still on screen, the note editor is closed, and no sheet, alert, cover or paywall is up, Settings' write-review row ends the asks, feedback never does, never in onboarding. Apple still caps the prompt at 3 a year. AGENTS.md's star prompt line names both moments.

Tests: `ReviewPromptTests` now covers any own finish asking (slower than the best, and an only race), a DNF not asking, another racer's career not asking, a transition-only best not counting as a personal best, and the personal best predicate for a non-best and an only race. Gate tests are unchanged.
