IM Tri Tracker is a read-only viewer for published race results. There are
no accounts and no sign-in.

Core flow:
1. Launch: the first screen asks for the athlete's name
2. Type a name. The app searches published results and lists matches.
3. Tap a match to claim it. The locker fills with that athlete's races.
4. Tap a race for splits, ranks, and field percentiles.
5. Bests tab ranks the athlete's races by swim, bike, run, or transitions.
6. Resume tab previews the free history and opens Race Book for comparison and export.

To review without a name of your own, search "Daniel Winek", a public
result set with five full-distance finishes.

The free core includes search, full history, splits, rankings and basic
race details. Race Book is a one-time lifetime purchase for comparison
and unlimited PDF/image export. There is no subscription or per-export
charge. Existing Iron Splits+ customers can restore prior access.

Data: results come from the public results service used by the events and
are shown as published. The app is independent and claims no affiliation
with any race organiser. Race names appear only to identify the results
they belong to.

Verified: light and dark mode, including accessibility text sizes.
No accounts, no social features, no user-generated content shared
between users.
