IM Iron Splits is a read-only viewer for published full and half-distance triathlon results. There are no accounts or sign-in.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Launch to Athlete Search. Search "Daniel Winek" and select the public result set.
2. After claiming, the Locker shows published results. Switch between Races and Rankings; rankings keep full and half distance separate.
3. Open a race for official splits, division and overall places, and field context.
4. Explore searches another athlete without changing the Locker.
5. Race Book shows free career totals, personal bests and progression. Compare and export actions open the in-app purchase sheet.
6. Settings contains Restore Purchases.

Search supports full registered names. Common names can be narrowed with a city or state after a comma. The broader fallback explains its longer wait and can be stopped.

Search, complete supported history, rankings, personal bests, progression, field context, notes and race details are free. Race Book is a one-time non-consumable purchase for like-for-like comparison and unlimited PDF or image export. There is no subscription or per-export charge. Nothing is sold outside the App Store.

Name searches and public contact/event identifiers are sent to the timing service to request published results. Race notes stay on the device. RevenueCat processes purchase and restore details and limited purchase-screen counts tied to an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat does not receive names, race results or race notes.

IM Iron Splits is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any race organiser or timing company. Screenshots use fictional sample results; the in-app search flow can use the public sample profile above.
