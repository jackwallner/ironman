IM Tri Tracker is a read-only viewer for published race results. There are
no accounts and no sign-in.

BUSINESS MODEL (answering the four questions on submission 64d6fd5a)

1. Who uses the paid feature: ordinary users of this app, on their own
   device. There is no other product, no membership, no team or organisation
   plan, and no group of users given access anywhere else.

2. Where it is purchased: only inside this app, only through In-App Purchase.
   "Race Book" (com.jackwallner.ironman.pro) is a one-time non-consumable,
   submitted for review with this version. It is not sold on a website or any
   other channel.

3. Previously purchased content a user can access: only that same In-App
   Purchase, reattached with Apple's restore flow (Settings, Race Book,
   Restore purchases) after a reinstall or on a new device. Nothing bought
   outside the App Store can unlock anything in the app.

4. Paid content unlocked without In-App Purchase: none.
   Build 13 was our mistake and is why this looked ambiguous. It was archived
   with a temporary compile-time flag left over from TestFlight that opened
   Race Book for everyone, so the Settings screen read "Race Book is
   unlocked" with no purchase made. That flag is removed in this build.
   Race Book now opens only after a completed or restored purchase.

The free core is deliberate and unchanged: search, the athlete's full race
history, splits, rankings and race details cost nothing. Race Book is the
only paid boundary and it adds two things, like-for-like race comparison and
unlimited PDF/image export.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Launch: the first screen asks for the athlete's name.
2. Search "Daniel Winek", a public result set with five full-distance
   finishes, and tap the match to claim it.
3. Locker fills with that athlete's races. Tap one for splits, ranks and
   field percentiles. All free.
4. Bests tab ranks those races by swim, bike, run or transitions. Free.
5. Resume tab, Race Book section: "Unlock race comparison" and "Unlock to
   export" open the purchase sheet. That sheet is the only way to unlock.
6. Settings, Race Book: the same purchase entry point, plus Restore
   purchases.

Data: results come from the public results service the events publish
through, shown as published. The app is independent and claims no
affiliation with any race organiser. Race names appear only to identify the
results they belong to.

Verified in light and dark mode, including accessibility text sizes. No
accounts, no social features, no user-generated content shared between
users.
