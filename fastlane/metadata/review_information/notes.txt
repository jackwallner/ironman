IM Tri Tracker is a read-only viewer for published full and half-distance
triathlon results. There are no accounts or sign-in.

Core flow:
1. Launch to Athlete Search. Search "Daniel Winek" and select the
   public result set.
2. After claiming, the Locker shows published results. Switch between Races and
   Rankings; rankings keep full and half distance separate.
3. Open a race for official splits, division and overall places, and
   field context.
4. Explore searches another athlete without changing the Locker.
5. Race Book shows free career totals, personal bests and progression.
   Compare and export actions open the in-app purchase sheet.
6. Settings contains Restore Purchases.

Search supports full registered names. Common names can be narrowed
with a city or state after a comma. The broad fallback explains its
longer wait and can be stopped.

Search, complete supported history, rankings, personal bests,
progression, field context, notes and race details are free. Race Book
is a one-time lifetime purchase for like-for-like comparison and
unlimited PDF/image export. There is no subscription or per-export
charge.

Data: name searches and public contact/event identifiers are sent to
the timing service to request results. Race notes stay on the device.
RevenueCat processes purchase and restore details and limited
purchase-screen counts tied to an anonymous app user ID. It receives
no names, race results or notes.

The app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any
race organiser or timing company. Screenshots use fictional sample
results; the in-app search flow can use the public sample profile above.
