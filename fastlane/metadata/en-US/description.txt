Every race you have ever finished, in one place, ranked the way you actually think about it.

Type your name once. IM Iron Splits finds you in the official results and fills your locker: swim, T1, bike, T2 and run splits, bib numbers, age-group and overall places, going back to your first start. No accounts, no manual entry, no digging through a different results page for every race.

YOUR SPLITS, RANKED
See every race you have done ranked by swim, by bike, by run, or by time lost in transition. Personal bests are marked on every leg, and each race shows how far off your best it was. Full-distance and half-distance are ranked separately, because comparing them tells you nothing.

WHERE YOU LANDED
Each split shown against the field that actually raced it. A rank means nothing without the size of the field behind it, so you get both, plus the percentile.

RACE RESUME
The sheet other races ask you to produce: date, distance, bib, official time and division place, for every start. Share it as text or a PDF in one tap.

RACE NOTES
Keep the conditions, the nutrition and the gear that produced each result attached to the result itself.

TRI POINTERS
A coaching clip library, filed by leg of the race.

FREE AND PRO
Search, claim your results, and see your three most recent races with full splits for free. Iron Splits+ unlocks your complete history, the split leaderboards, field percentiles, race notes, the resume export and the full Pointers library.
