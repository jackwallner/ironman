IM Tri Tracker finds your published full and half-distance triathlon results by name and ranks each swim, bike and run split against the field from that race. Results, splits, rankings and personal bests are free, with no account and no subscription.

FIND YOUR RACES
• Search the name you registered with to find your supported published results
• Each result shows swim, T1, bike, T2, run and finish times, bib number, division place and overall rank
• Your claimed races stay together in one Locker

SPLIT RANKINGS
• Rank your races by swim, bike, run, transitions or finish time
• Full-distance and half-distance races are ranked separately
• Each split shows its rank, the field size and your percentile in that race

PERSONAL BESTS
• Personal bests and progression across your races

RACE NOTES
• Notes on conditions, nutrition and gear, kept with the result they belong to

EXPLORE
• Look up another athlete's full and half-distance race history without changing your own Locker

TIPS FROM PATTIE
• A library of filmed race-day tips
• Ask Pattie points you to the right clip for what you are working on

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking
• Your Locker, race notes and recently viewed athletes stay on your iPhone
• Name searches go to the timing service that publishes the results

FREE AND RACE BOOK
Everything above is free and does not expire. Race Book is an optional one-time purchase with no subscription, and you can preview it before you buy. It adds:
• Like-for-like comparisons between races
• Unlimited PDF and image exports of your race history, with splits, career stats and optional notes

IM Tri Tracker is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any race organizer or timing company. Results are shown as published by each event's timer.
