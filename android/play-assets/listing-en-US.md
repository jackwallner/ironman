# Google Play store listing, en-US

**App name:** IM Tri Tracker: Triathlon

**Short description:** Triathlon results by name: race splits, age group ranks, and personal bests.

## Full description

Look up your triathlon results by name and see every race split ranked. IM Tri Tracker finds published full-distance and half-distance triathlon results and turns them into a race history you can read at a glance.

Search the name you raced under. Keep your own triathlon results in an on-device Locker, or Explore another athlete's published race results: a training partner, a rival, or a friend you tracked on race day.

SEE EVERY SPLIT
Swim, T1, bike, T2, run, and finish times for each triathlon, with bib number, age group, and overall, gender, and age group ranks when the timer published them. DNF, DNS, and DQ results are marked, so they never count as a fast split.

RANK YOUR SPLITS BY DISTANCE
Rank your swim splits, bike splits, run splits, transitions, and finish times against every race you have done at the same distance. Full-distance and half-distance triathlons stay in separate rankings, and each split shows its gap to your personal best.

AGAINST THE FIELD
See where each split placed among the finishers of the same event, with rank and percentile for swim, bike, run, and finish.

RACE NOTES
Save course conditions, nutrition, and gear notes with the race result that produced them. Your Locker and notes stay on this device.

TRIATHLON TIPS FROM PATTIE
Browse a library of race-day pointers for swim, bike, run, and transitions, or follow Ask Pattie, a guided path to her recorded advice for the moment you are in. Episodes download before playback and can be watched offline.

RACE BOOK
Compare races like for like and export unlimited PDF or image race reports with one lifetime purchase. Google Play shows the price before purchase. There is no subscription or per-export charge.

FREE CORE
Triathlon results search, your race history, splits, split rankings, field context, race details, notes, and the complete tips library stay free.

WHO IT IS FOR
Age group triathletes racing long course and middle distance events, coaches checking an athlete's race splits, and friends and family who want a finisher's results after the race.

DATA AND INDEPENDENCE
A search sends the name you enter to the third-party timing service that returns published results. Your Locker, race notes, and recent Explore profiles stay on this device. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

IM Tri Tracker is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any race organizer or timing company. Results are shown as published by each event timer. Only full-distance and half-distance triathlon results are shown.

IM Tri Tracker is for general race and fitness information only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

## Search notes

Play has no keyword field. The title, short description, and full description
are indexed, so the head terms live there: `triathlon` (title), `triathlon
results`, `race splits`, `age group`, `personal best` (short description), and
`swim/bike/run splits`, `race results`, `half-distance`, `long course` (full
description). Keep core terms near 2 to 3 percent density; more reads as
keyword stuffing. The launcher name stays `IM Tri Tracker`; only the Play title
carries the `: Triathlon` descriptor. Never add trademarked event names or
distance shorthand as keywords.

## App content

- Category: Sports
- Ads: No
- Target audience: Adults 18 and older only. Restrict users Google has determined to be minors.
- App access: No account or special reviewer access is needed. Most features are free; Race Book unlocks after a Google Play test purchase.
- User data: The app sends searched names and public result identifiers to the timing service. RevenueCat processes Google Play purchase and entitlement data with an anonymous app user ID. Local athlete data, recent profiles, and notes are not uploaded by the app.
- Account deletion: No developer account system exists. Local data is removed by clearing the Locker or uninstalling the app. Contact Support to request deletion of an anonymous RevenueCat customer record.
- Health apps declaration: Activity and fitness only. No medical-device features, diagnosis, or treatment claims.
