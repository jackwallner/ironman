### Why the visible fields change

`IM Tri Tracker` alone does not tell a new visitor whether the app tracks training, live athletes, races, workouts, GPS activity, or published results. `Race Results` resolves the category at the highest-weight visible level.

`Triathlon Splits & Rankings` names the differentiated mechanism:

- `Triathlon` prevents the broad `race results` phrase from looking like motorsport or event-timing software.
- `Splits` states the swim, transition, bike, and run evidence.
- `Rankings` states how the app interprets those splits against the field and personal history.

The ampersand is visible punctuation for readability. Apple can combine tokens across the title, subtitle, and hidden field, so no keyword-field duplication is needed.

### Current result intent

`triathlon` is dominated by USA Triathlon, TrainingPeaks, the official IRONMAN Tracker, training plans, coaching, event apps, and live video. A generic triathlon searcher is more likely to expect training or live tracking than historical result analysis.

`race results` is broad and polluted by timing-company utilities, motorsport, live race tracking, calendars, and race organizers. The visible `Triathlon` qualifier is essential.

`triathlon splits` is the most product-aligned result family, but it currently leads with TriExport, training tools, pace calculators, and race planners. Gist-like wording is not enough here. Screenshots must visibly prove that these are published result splits, not planned splits.

`personal best tracker` returns generic record, workout, track-and-field, habit, and GPS apps. Keep `personal best` as a compositional benefit, not the top-level category.

`race history` and `race splits` include car games, timers, pace planners, timing tools, and HYROX products. The title and subtitle must work together to qualify triathlon results.

## Positioning and claims

Recommended position:

> Find your published race results by name, then see every triathlon split, ranking, personal best, and comparable race in one private history

Safe claims, subject to current binary and feed verification:

- Search a name against the configured published-results feed
- Claim one athlete identity without an account
- Load supported full-distance, half-distance, running, and trail results from that feed
- See swim, T1, bike, T2, run, and finish times where published
- See bib, age-group place, overall place, field size, and percentile where available
- Rank races within comparable race kinds
- Mark personal bests by discipline
- Keep local race notes
- Preview Race Book progression and career summary
- Compare like-for-like races through the lifetime unlock
- Export a PDF or image through the lifetime unlock

Claim gates:

- `Every race` means every supported published result returned for the claimed identity, not every event the user has ever entered.
- `Full race record` requires a complete successful feed load and clear unsupported-event language.
- `Gained time` means a mathematical difference between same-distance results. It does not prove why the difference occurred.
- `Private` means the claimed locker and notes stay on the device. Published race facts are already public at the source.
- `Personal best` must compare like-for-like race kinds and valid nonzero splits.
